Delete Your ScoreWise Account & Data

Follow the instructions below to permanently delete your account and associated personal data from ScoreWise.

How to Request Account Deletion

Open the Contact Support Section

Navigate to the "Contact Support" section within the ScoreWise app. You can find this in the main menu dropdown.

Select Account Deletion

In the subject field, choose the "Account Deletion" option from the dropdown menu.

Enter Your Email Address

Provide the email address associated with your ScoreWise account to help us verify your identity.

Send Your Request

Submit your request. No additional message is required—we'll process it based on the email address you provide.

Tip: Make sure you're using the email address linked to your ScoreWise account to ensure we can locate and delete the correct data.

What Happens After You Submit

Verification & Processing

We will verify your request and begin processing it within 24 hours of submission.

Data Deletion

Upon verification, we will permanently delete the following personal data associated with your account:

  • Your account profile and preferences
  • Your registered email address
  • Your subscription and payment history
  • All saved personal data and app activity

Data Retention Notice

Please note that we may retain certain anonymous, aggregated data for analytical purposes as permitted by law. This data cannot be traced back to you personally.

Important Information

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your data is deleted, you will lose access to all ScoreWise features and your account cannot be restored.

If you have an active subscription, please cancel it through the Google Play Store before requesting account deletion.
